# Product Specification

Version 1 provides Puter authentication, PDF resume upload, job context capture,
AI analysis, ATS scoring, detailed feedback, per-user history, and individual
review pages. It targets students, graduates, engineers, career switchers, and
resume reviewers.

Required inputs are company, job title, job description, required experience,
and one valid PDF. Required outputs are an overall score; ATS, tone, content,
structure, skills, and keyword scores; matched and missing keywords; strengths;
weaknesses; and actionable suggestions.

The application is responsive, accessible, type-safe, serverless, and requires
no API secrets. Resume building, cover letters, interview preparation, recruiter
features, analytics, and collaboration are outside version 1.
